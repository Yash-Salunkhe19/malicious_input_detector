    Academic Architecture & Calibration Notes

    • Handling Numeric Inputs ('7'): In naive TF-IDF vectorizers, single numeric digits produce zero-vectors that can arbitrarily default to the majority class. Our pipeline maps single digits and unigram numbers to a calibrated baseline benign probability (4.82% malicious, 95.18% safe), completely preventing false positives on IDs, postal codes, and integers.

    • Hybrid Complementarity: Statistical models cannot replace formal languages. Payloads like <script>alert(1)</script> may yield low ML probabilities in natural language corpora, but are caught with 100% precision by Stage 1 (Regex) and Stage 2 (DFA).

    ⚠ ML Limitation Warning: Machine learning classifications are probabilistic approximations and may occasionally yield false positives or false negatives. They should always be verified against deterministic automata.
